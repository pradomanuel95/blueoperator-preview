When something on your pool stops working, you want it fixed right, and you want to know what you're paying for before we start. We handle the most common pool equipment problems around Miami-Dade, so your system runs smoothly and your water stays safe.

Most repair calls start the same way: the pump is loud or won't prime, the filter pressure is off, there's a drip at the equipment pad, or the salt system is showing an error. We come out, find the cause and explain it in plain English (or Spanish). Then we give you a clear estimate. Nothing extra gets done without your OK.

Typical repairs include:

  • Pump repairs (motor, seals, priming issues)
  • Filter repairs (leaks, pressure problems, media replacement)
  • Valves, gaskets, and o-rings
  • Plumbing above ground (fittings, unions, leaks)
  • Salt system troubleshooting and cleaning

Replacements and installations

Sometimes a repair isn't worth it, and a new piece of equipment will save you money and headaches. When that's the case, we'll tell you straight and help you pick the right option for your pool. We replace and install:

  • Heat pumps and gas heaters
  • Single speed and variable speed pumps
  • Salt systems
  • Sand, cartridge and DE filters
  • Automation systems

Pool repair FAQs

What pool equipment do you repair?

We work on pumps, filters, valves, gaskets and o-rings, above-ground plumbing, and salt systems. We also replace and install heat pumps, gas heaters, pumps, salt systems, filters and automation systems.

Will I know the cost before you start?

Yes. We find the cause first, then give you a clear estimate. You always get an estimate before any additional work is done.

Should I repair my pump or replace it?

It depends on the problem and the condition of the pump. We'll check it and tell you honestly which makes more sense for your pool, without pushing a replacement you don't need.

Do you do resurfacing or pool construction?

No. If your pool needs resurfacing or construction, we can refer you to partners we trust.
