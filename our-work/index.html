Frequently asked questions

Are these real pools you service?

Yes. Every photo is a real Blue Operator pool we cleaned or currently service. The photos are only cropped, never edited. We show the neighborhood and ZIP only, to protect client privacy.

How do you bring a green pool back?

We brush the pool, balance the water, and filter it until it’s clear again. If stains are still on the surface once the water has cleared, we can look at an acid or chlorine wash. After the recovery, weekly service is what keeps it from sliding back.

What’s included in weekly service?

Weekly service includes cleaning (skim, brush, vacuum, and empty the baskets), water testing and balancing, standard chemicals, an equipment check, a photo report after each visit, and monthly filter cleaning — or earlier if the pool needs it. You can see the full list on our Weekly Pool Service page.

Do you service salt and chlorine pools?

Yes. We service both salt and chlorine pools, and we check the salt system on every visit.

How do I get a quote?

You can request a quote online, or call or text 305-925-4491. We quote from your photos and measure the pool from Google Maps, so we don’t need to come out first. We speak English and Spanish.
