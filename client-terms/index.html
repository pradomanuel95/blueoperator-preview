Terms of Service

Last Updated 04/22/2026

Effective 01/01/2026

This agreement is made between BLUE OPERATOR INC. and the CUSTOMER.

The purpose of this agreement is for BLUE OPERATOR INC. to perform pool and/or spa maintenance and/or repairs as described below, to the CUSTOMER for a fee, to be paid as described below.

1. Conditions of Service

1 - The frequency of service to be provided by BLUE OPERATOR INC. is weekly only. BLUE OPERATOR INC. does not offer bi-weekly or alternate service frequencies. Our service guarantees up to 48 visits per year.

2 - All service options require the CUSTOMER to maintain the pool/spa in between routine service visits. This may include but is not limited to skimming the pool and emptying traps (skimmer, pump and vacuum baskets, etc.). Failure to perform standard tasks may result in unbalanced chemistry resulting in unsafe swimming conditions and increased cost to the CUSTOMER.

3 - The CUSTOMER represents that he/she is legally able to receive work at the address provided upon which the pool/spa work is to be performed and that no existing covenants or restrictions hinder the performance of such work.

4 - Under any and all circumstances the CUSTOMER shall furnish and be responsible for the cost related to furnishing water, fuel and power for the services and maintenance of their pool/spa.

5 - All services require CUSTOMER to maintain proper water levels in pool/spa. BLUE OPERATOR INC. will not be held responsible for any damages to the pool/spa, equipment or any other issues that arise as a result of a low water level in a pool/spa. If water levels are not adequate, BLUE OPERATOR INC. will make a note in the weekly report to notify the CUSTOMER and may turn off the circulation equipment if deemed necessary by BLUE OPERATOR INC. to protect CUSTOMER’S equipment. The water level should stand at a minimum 1/3 the height of the skimmer (weir) entrance. Anything below this minimum is considered low.

6 - Please note: BLUE OPERATOR INC. will make every effort to accommodate the CUSTOMER’S preferred day of the week. However, BLUE OPERATOR INC. reserves the right to perform its services on another day, if necessary. The service technician is not required to communicate the right to perform these services on another day.

2. Service Access & Scheduling

Pool service is performed within a weekday service window (Monday–Friday). While we aim for consistency, the exact service day may vary due to weather, route optimization, or operational needs. The CUSTOMER agrees to provide unlocked access to the pool area on all weekdays. If access is unavailable, service may be skipped or rescheduled, and a trip fee may apply.

3. Inclement Weather

In the event of rain on your service day, the pool/spa will be cleaned to the extent weather permits. BLUE OPERATOR INC. has the right to not vacuum the pool if the technician cannot see to the bottom of the pool/spa due to the inclement weather to protect his/her equipment from damage.

4. Missed Service Calls

In the event that BLUE OPERATOR INC. needs to reschedule a visit that was scheduled specifically by the CUSTOMER, a 24-hour notice will be given in addition to another day that would be agreed to work better for both parties. It is expected of the CUSTOMER to extend the same courtesy if service call needs to be rescheduled.

5. Pool Access & Service Readiness

The CUSTOMER agrees to provide clear and safe access to the pool area, including an unobstructed path for equipment and adequate space around the pool. Pool toys, floats, hoses, and loose items must be removed from the pool prior to service, as submerged items may prevent vacuuming or damage equipment. Pets must be secured during service. Service quality may be impacted if the pool area is not service-ready.

6. Holidays

BLUE OPERATOR INC. will observe the following holidays and services may or may not be made up based on scheduling availability:

New Year’s Day

Independence Day

Thanksgiving Day

Christmas Day

7. Pool Equipment Operation Requirements

The CUSTOMER is responsible for proper pool circulation and equipment operation. From April–September, the pump must run a minimum of 8 hours per day, and from October–March, a minimum of 6 hours per day, with run time occurring primarily during daylight hours. Return jets must be operational and directed slightly downward to promote circulation. Additional services or adjustments required due to insufficient run time or circulation may result in additional charges.

8. Services to be Performed

BLUE OPERATOR INC. will furnish all materials, including routine maintenance chemicals, for the monthly service packages or the chosen one-time service. Additional service visits may be needed and billed in addition to the normal service fees (storms, severe algae problems, phosphates, small animal removal, acts of nature, debris removal caused by another company, etc.). All services are performed as needed and by the discretion of BLUE OPERATOR INC. pending approval by the CUSTOMER.

Routine weekly service is designed to maintain pools under normal conditions and includes typical debris such as leaves, dust and insects. Excessive debris caused by storms, landscaping, construction, heavy leaf fall, or other abnormal conditions is not included and may require additional service at an extra cost, subject to CUSTOMER approval.

Any algae growth or water deterioration occurring while service is paused, delayed, or ceased may require additional service and will be billed accordingly.

9. Pricing

The CUSTOMER agrees to pay BLUE OPERATOR INC. the amount specified agreed upon in a prepay format. Any additional service or repairs performed by BLUE OPERATOR INC. will be priced based on the job needed to be completed and invoiced to the CUSTOMER. The prices given for additional services will include parts and/or chemicals. BLUE OPERATOR INC. will not perform additional service or repairs until approved by the CUSTOMER.

10. Billing

The CUSTOMER will receive their invoice from BLUE OPERATOR INC. via email on or around the first (1st) of each month to be due on the first (1st) of that same month. This is a prepay cycle so that payment due on the first of the month will be for that month of service.

11. Payment

BLUE OPERATOR INC. accepts credit cards for all services. Monthly service invoices paid by credit card are subject to a 3.05% processing fee. Payments made via Zelle do not incur additional fees. Repair services are not subject to credit card processing fees.

12. Initial Startup Visit

It is important the CUSTOMER’S pool be clean, algae free and chemically balanced when monthly service begins. If the pool has algae, cloudy water, excessive debris or anything else that cannot be taken care of during the course of a normal service visit, there will be additional fees assessed prior to normal weekly service starting.

13. Cease Service Policy

The invoice is due on the first (1st) of the month for that month of service (prepay). Reminders are automatically sent until the invoice is paid. If the invoice is still left unpaid by the fifteenth (15th), service will stop until the invoice has been paid.

Any chemical deficiencies or other issues that arise after service has ceased will incur an additional cost to the CUSTOMER to correct. BLUE OPERATOR INC. will not be held responsible for issues that arise after service has been stopped due to non-payment.

14. Chemicals / Filter Media & Cleanings

Routine maintenance chemicals are added each service and are furnished by BLUE OPERATOR INC. For any additional services, the CUSTOMER will be charged for the parts, chemicals and/or labor. Additional chemicals can be purchased upon request of the CUSTOMER.

Filter cleaning frequency depends on the CUSTOMER’S selected service tier and may be performed monthly or quarterly, or more frequently if necessary. Replacing filter media will require written or verbal approval from the CUSTOMER.

15. Disclaimer

BLUE OPERATOR INC. will not be responsible for any damage to property due to acts of nature, fire, vandalism, God, war, riot, misuse or abuse by the CUSTOMER. BLUE OPERATOR INC. is not responsible for the performance of any chemicals it does not provide. The CUSTOMER should be aware of normal deterioration of equipment that occurs over time due to exposure to chemicals, temperature, sunlight, etc.

16. Saltwater Pools & Salt Systems

For saltwater pools, up to three (3) bags of salt per month are included with weekly service; any additional salt required will be billed separately. Salt cell cleaning is performed quarterly (every three months) and is not included in the regular service rate; salt cell cleanings are billed separately.

17. Termination

This agreement may be terminated by the CUSTOMER with a thirty (30) day written or verbal notice as long as the CUSTOMER’S balance is paid in full. BLUE OPERATOR INC. reserves the right to withdraw services if customer satisfaction cannot be achieved.

18. Our Agreement

Both the CUSTOMER and BLUE OPERATOR INC. agree that this written agreement contains all agreements and understandings. No amendments or changes shall be valid unless made in writing and approved by both parties.

BLUE OPERATOR INC. reserves the right to update these Terms of Service. Updated terms will apply to services provided after the effective date and will be made available to the CUSTOMER.

19. Warranty

BLUE OPERATOR INC. warrants that all material used will be of good quality and workmanship. Any defects must be reported within twenty-four (24) hours of service completion.

20. Photography & Video

BLUE OPERATOR INC. may take photos or videos of the pool, equipment, or work being performed on the property for documentation, training, and marketing purposes, including use on our website and social media.

Media will be limited to the pool area only. We do not intentionally record people, personal areas, or identifying information.

By using our services, the client grants permission for this use. Clients may opt out by notifying us in writing prior to service.

21. Pump Operation Schedule

To ensure proper chemical circulation and filtration after service, Blue Operator may adjust pool pump run times when needed. Our recommended standard circulation window on service days is 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Clients may request to maintain a different schedule; however, reduced circulation after service may affect chemical effectiveness and debris removal. In such cases, Blue Operator may temporarily leave the pump running after service when necessary to support proper treatment.
