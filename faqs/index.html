FAQ's

Frequently asked questions

General

Why hire a professional pool service?

Owning a pool should be effortless. With Blue Operator, you don’t worry about chemistry, algae, or equipment problems. We handle everything so your water stays clear, protected, and safe. Most clients save time, stress, and long-term repair costs by having a professional maintain their pool properly.

Are chemicals included?

Yes, all standard chemicals (chlorine, acid, stabilizer, etc.) are included with every plan.
Specialty chemicals (phosphate removers, enzymes, clarifiers) are billed separately.
BlueProtect+ clients get 30% off all specialty chemical treatments.

How often do you service my pool?

Pools are serviced once a week on a consistent schedule.
If you need a special visit (party, guests, photo shoot, etc.), we do our best to fit you in.

What's the difference between BlueProtect and BlueProtect Plus?

BlueProtect includes weekly cleaning, water balancing, and equipment checks.
BlueProtect Plus adds premium benefits:

  • Extended plaster protection (enhanced calcium balance)
  • 30% off specialty chemical treatments (like phosphate removers)
  • 30% off salt cell cleanings
  • Priority response for repairs and storms
  • Monthly filter cleanings
Do you clean the filter? How often?

Yes:

  • BlueProtect: monthly (or earlier if needed)
  • BlueProtect+: monthly (or earlier if needed)

We adjust based on storms, bather load, or pool conditions.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No long-term contracts.

We work month-to-month.

You’ll simply sign a short terms-of-service form so expectations are clear and transparent.

How do storms affect service?

Florida storms can quickly affect water balance and equipment.

We prioritize BlueProtect+ clients first for post-storm cleanups and equipment issues, then service the remaining schedule as quickly as possible.

Are there extra costs not included in monthly service?

Most maintenance is included. Extras apply only to:

  • Specialty chemicals (30% off on BlueProtect+)
  • Salt cell cleanings (30% off on BlueProtect+)
  • Major storm cleanups
  • Repairs or installations

You always receive an estimate before any additional work is done.

What repairs or installations do you handle?

We service and install:

  • Pumps
  • Filters
  • Salt systems
  • Timers
  • Automation Systems
  • Heat Pumps
  • Gas Heaters
  • Above ground plumbing leaks

If your pool needs resurfacing or construction, we can refer trusted partners.

How quickly can you start?

Most clients are added within 24–48 hours.
During peak season, timing may vary slightly, but we always onboard new clients as quickly as possible.

How do I know if my pool is leaking?

Check the water level in the morning and again at night.

If you’re losing about ⅓ inch or more per day (without heavy use), you may have a leak.

We can inspect the system and help determine next steps.

What areas do you service?

We currently serve homeowners across Miami. Primarily Kendall, West Kendall, Coral Gables, Pinecrest, Cutler Bay and surrounding neighborhoods.

What if my pool has a unique issue?

Just reach out. Whether it's cloudy water, stains, equipment errors, or something unusual, we’ll help diagnose it and provide the right solution.

Is it easy to reach you?

Yes, we’re committed to fast, clear communication.
Text, call, or email anytime. If we miss your call while servicing a pool, we respond promptly.
