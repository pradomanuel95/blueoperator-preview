Our Story

Before starting Blue Operator, I worked in pool supplies. Every week people would come in frustrated; their pool guy had stopped showing up, quit without notice, or given them bad advice on chemicals that made the problem worse. Frequently asked questions

General

Are chemicals included?

Yes, all standard chemicals (chlorine, acid, stabilizer, etc.) are included with every plan. Specialty chemicals (phosphate removers, enzymes, clarifiers) are billed separately.

How often do you service my pool?

Pools are serviced once a week on a consistent schedule. If you need a special visit (party, guests, photo shoot, etc.), we do our best to fit you in.

What’s the difference between BlueProtect and BlueProtect Plus?

BlueProtect includes weekly cleaning, water balancing, and equipment checks. BlueProtect Plus adds premium benefits:

  • Extended plaster protection (enhanced calcium balance)
  • Priority response for repairs and storms
  • Monthly filter cleanings
Do I need to sign a contract?

No long-term contracts. We work month-to-month. You’ll simply sign a short terms-of-service form so expectations are clear and transparent.

Are there extra costs not included in monthly service?

Most maintenance is included. Extras apply only to:

  • Specialty chemicals
  • Salt cell cleanings
  • Major storm cleanups
  • Repairs or installations

You always receive an estimate before any additional work is done.

How quickly can you start?

Most clients are added within 24–48 hours. During peak season, timing may vary slightly, but we always onboard new clients as quickly as possible.
