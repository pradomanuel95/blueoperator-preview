Local Pool Service in The Hammocks

The Hammocks is one of West Kendall’s best-known planned communities, built around a chain of lakes, walking trails, and parks between SW 88th Street and SW 120th Street. We take care of home pools all through it, from Parkside, Lakeside, and Pelican Point to Live Oak and Spicewood, and the streets near Hammocks Boulevard and West Kendall Baptist Hospital. If you’re in 33186 or 33196, you’re in our Hammocks service area.

Learn more about The Hammocks on Wikipedia (Wikidata). Area codes: 305 / 786.

Most of The Hammocks was built in the 1970s and ’80s, so a lot of pools here have years on the finish and the equipment. Summer sun burns off chlorine fast, and almost-daily afternoon storms dilute the water and wash in debris. When the chemistry drifts, it’s not only the water that suffers: unbalanced water etches plaster and Diamond Brite, leaves scale on tile, and wears down pumps, heaters, and salt cells.

Our weekly service stops that before it starts. Chemicals are included, we clean your filter every month, and we come on the same day each week whenever possible, backed by our No Missed Stop Guarantee and No Green Guarantee. If something breaks, we repair pumps, filters, and salt systems, and we also offer acid and chlorine washes and pool leak detection in The Hammocks.

We’re insured, and we speak English and Spanish. Text us a few photos of your pool for a quote. Call or text 305-925-4491. We also serve nearby pool service in Kendall, weekly pool cleaning in Country Walk, and weekly pool cleaning in Kendall West.

The Hammocks Pool Service FAQs

How much is weekly pool service in The Hammocks?

It depends on your pool: its size, your equipment (salt or chlorine, pump and filter), and its condition. Chemicals and monthly filter cleaning are always included, with no surprise charges. Text us a photo or your address and we’ll send your price by phone or text. See what’s included on our Weekly Pool Service page.

Do you service pools in my part of The Hammocks?

Yes. We take care of in-ground home pools all over The Hammocks, including Parkside, Lakeside, Pelican Point, Live Oak, and Spicewood. If you’re in 33186 or 33196, call or text 305-925-4491 or fill out the form above.

Will you come on the same day every week?

Yes. Whenever possible, your pool stays on the same service day every week, and our No Missed Stop Guarantee backs it up.

What’s included in weekly service?

Every visit we brush, skim, vacuum, and empty the baskets, then test and balance the water. Chemicals are included, we clean your filter every month, and we check your equipment each visit.
