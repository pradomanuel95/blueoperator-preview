If your pool is losing more water than normal evaporation, there may be a leak in the plumbing or circulation system. A small leak can waste a lot of water, raise your water bill and, if it's left alone, damage the ground and equipment around your pool. We provide professional pool leak detection using pressure testing and isolation methods to find the source of the water loss.

Our leak detection services include:

  • Pressure testing of underground plumbing lines
  • Isolation of suction and return lines
  • Equipment pad and valve inspections
  • Skimmer, return, and fitting evaluations
  • Identification of leaks causing abnormal water loss

Is it a leak or just evaporation?

In Miami's sun and heat, some water loss is normal. Here's a simple way to check: look at the water level in the morning and again at night. If you're losing about ⅓ inch or more per day without heavy use, you may have a leak. Other signs are wet spots near the equipment pad, the pump pulling air, or needing to add water more often than usual.

Why not wait?

Leaks rarely fix themselves. Water that escapes underground can soften the soil around your pool and equipment, and topping off all the time throws off your water chemistry. The longer the pool keeps losing water, the more it costs in water, chemicals and possible damage. Finding the leak early usually means a simpler fix.

Leak detection FAQs

How do I know if my pool is leaking?

Check the water level in the morning and again at night. If you're losing about ⅓ inch or more per day without heavy use, you may have a leak. Call us and we'll help you figure it out.

How do you find the leak?

We pressure test the underground plumbing lines, isolate the suction and return lines, and inspect the equipment pad, valves, skimmers, returns and fittings until we find where the water is going.

Can you fix the leak once you find it?

Leaks in above-ground plumbing, fittings and equipment are part of our repair services. We'll explain what we found and give you an estimate before any work is done.

How much does leak detection cost?
