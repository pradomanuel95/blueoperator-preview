Kendale Lakes grew up around the old Kendale Lakes Golf & Country Club, today’s Miccosukee Golf & Country Club, and it’s still a neighborhood of shady streets, canals, and lakes. We take care of home pools from the streets around the golf course and Kendale Lakes Park to the neighborhoods near Kendall Soccer Park and Miami Sunset High. Kendale Lakes was one of the first big developments out west, started in the late 1960s, so plenty of pools here are as old as the homes. Older pools usually mean resurfaced or patched plaster, original plumbing, and pumps that have been running for a long time. The big shade trees that make these streets so pleasant also drop leaves into the water, and summer heat and storms keep pushing the chemistry off.

That’s why we focus on balance, not just clear water. The right chemistry protects your plaster or Diamond Brite finish and keeps scale and corrosion away from your equipment. Chemicals are included in our weekly service, we clean your filter monthly, and we come on the same day each week whenever possible, backed by our No Missed Stop Guarantee and No Green Guarantee. How much is weekly pool service in Kendale Lakes?

It depends on your pool’s size, equipment, and condition. Your price always includes chemicals and monthly filter cleaning, with no surprise charges. Send a photo or your address and we’ll quote you by phone or text. See what’s included on our Weekly Pool Service page.

Do you service pools in my part of Kendale Lakes?

Yes. We take care of in-ground home pools all over Kendale Lakes, from the golf course area to the streets around Kendale Lakes Park and Kendall Soccer Park. If you’re in 33175 or 33183, call or text 305-925-4491 or fill out the form above.

My pool is older. Can you keep it in good shape?

Yes. Balanced water is the best protection an older pool can get, because it keeps the finish from etching and the equipment from scaling or corroding. If the surface is already stained, an acid or chlorine wash brings it back. If the water keeps dropping, we do leak detection, and we repair pumps, filters, and salt systems.
