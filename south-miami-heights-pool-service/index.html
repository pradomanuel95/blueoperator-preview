Local Pool Service in South Miami Heights

South Miami Heights, first known as Eureka, is a large residential community just south of Zoo Miami and Larry and Penny Thompson Park. Big parts of it were built around the early 1970s, with street after street of single-family homes. We take care of home pools all over it, from the streets along Eureka Drive (SW 184th Street) and around Eureka Park to the neighborhoods near Miami Southridge High School, Coach Sam Burley Park, and Roberta Hunter Park. If you’re in 33177 or 33157, you're in our South Miami Heights service area.

Learn more about South Miami Heights on Wikipedia (Wikidata). Area codes: 305 / 786.

Many homes here are decades old, and a pool that’s been in the ground that long needs careful chemistry to protect its finish and equipment. Out here the summer sun is intense, and afternoon storms roll through almost every day, burning off chlorine and pushing the pH around. Water that stays out of balance etches plaster and Diamond Brite, leaves scale on the tile, and is hard on pumps, filters, and salt cells.

Our weekly service is built to protect what you have. Chemicals are included, filters are cleaned every month, and we come on the same day each week whenever possible. If something breaks, we repair pumps, filters, and salt systems, and we also do acid and chlorine washes and leak detection.

We’re insured, and we speak English and Spanish. Text a photo of your pool to 305-925-4491, or send your address and we’ll measure it on Google Maps. New clients get $30 off their first month. We also serve nearby weekly pool cleaning in Cutler Bay, weekly pool cleaning in Palmetto Bay, and weekly pool cleaning in Country Walk.

Do you service pools in my part of South Miami Heights?

Yes. We take care of in-ground home pools all over South Miami Heights, from the Eureka Drive area to the neighborhoods around Southridge. If you’re in 33177 or 33157, call or text 305-925-4491 or fill out the form above.

Do you service saltwater pools?

Yes. We take care of both salt and chlorine pools. On salt pools we keep the salt level and the rest of the chemistry balanced, and we repair salt systems when they stop working right.

Yes, whenever possible. We put your pool on a regular service day, and we’ll let you know if anything ever has to change.
