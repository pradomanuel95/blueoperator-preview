Our Story

Before starting Blue Operator, I worked in pool supplies. A lot of Gables homes are older, and so are their pools. Older plaster and tile, original plumbing, and aging equipment need a careful eye, because small leaks, worn surfaces, and stains tend to show up more often than in newer pools. The city's lush landscaping and big shade trees also drop leaves and debris into the water, and summer heat and storms push the chemistry off balance.

With weekly visits, we keep your water balanced and catch small problems before they grow, backed by our No Missed Stop Guarantee and No Green Guarantee.
