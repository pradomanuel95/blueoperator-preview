Why chemistry matters more than clear water

Clear water isn't always balanced water. When pH, alkalinity and calcium hardness drift, water becomes either aggressive or scaling. Aggressive water pulls calcium out of the surface, which shows up as etching, rough spots and a dull finish on plaster and Diamond Brite. Scaling water leaves crusty white deposits on tile, inside the heater and on the salt cell.

We test and balance every week and keep your water's saturation index (LSI) in the safe range, so the water isn't eating your surface or coating your equipment.

  • Plaster & Diamond Brite. Less etching and calcium loss, so the finish stays smooth and resurfacing gets pushed further out.
  • Pumps, heaters & salt cells. Balanced water means less scale, less corrosion, and equipment that lasts longer.
  • Tile line. Less calcium buildup at the waterline.

BlueProtect Plus goes a step further with advanced conditioning and mineral stabilization aimed at the plaster itself.

Weekly service FAQs

What's the difference between BlueProtect and BlueProtect Plus?

BlueProtect covers weekly cleaning, chemistry, monthly filter cleaning, an equipment check and a photo report. Plus adds surface-protection water conditioning, 30% off specialty chemicals and salt cell cleanings, priority after storms and faster response between visits.

How much does weekly pool service cost?

It depends on your pool's size, features and condition. Call or text 305-925-4491 with a few photos and we'll give you a quote.

Do you need to come out to give me a quote?

No. We quote from your photos and measure the pool from Google Maps.

How often do you clean the filter?

Monthly on both plans, or earlier or later if conditions or scheduling call for it.

Will I know what was done at each visit?

Yes. You get a detailed report with photos after every visit, and we'll text or email you if the water level is low or high or we spot an equipment issue.

Do you service salt pools?

Yes. We keep salt pools balanced and check the system every visit. BlueProtect Plus clients save 30% on salt cell cleanings.

What kinds of pools do you service?

Residential in-ground pools across Miami-Dade.

What happens after a storm?

We get pools back in shape after heavy rain or debris, and BlueProtect Plus clients get priority scheduling.

Do you clean salt cells?

Yes. We inspect your salt cell during service and clean it when scale builds up, so your salt system keeps making chlorine. If the cell is worn out, we can replace it.

Do you clean pool filters?

Yes. We clean cartridge and DE filters as part of keeping your water clear, and we'll let you know when a filter needs repair or replacement.
